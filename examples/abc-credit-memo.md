## Financial Analyst

- The fictional, unaudited FY2025 summary reports revenue of USD 120 million and EBITDA of USD 18 million. EBITDA margin is 18 / 120 = 15% for FY2025. [F1]
- Using total debt of USD 54 million and FY2025 EBITDA of USD 18 million, gross debt / EBITDA is 3.0x. Assuming the reported USD 9 million cash is unrestricted and available to offset debt, net debt is USD 45 million and net debt / FY2025 EBITDA is 2.5x; cash availability is unverified. [F1, D1]
- FY2025 EBITDA / interest expense is USD 18 million / USD 6 million = 3.0x. This is EBITDA interest coverage, not cash debt-service coverage: EBITDA does not establish cash available after working capital, capital expenditure, tax and principal payments. [F1]
- At the debt summary date of 2025-12-31, USD 30 million of USD 54 million total debt matures on 2026-12-31, representing 55.6% of total debt. In a static scenario assuming all reported USD 9 million cash remains available for repayment, cash covers 30% of that maturity and leaves USD 21 million to be met from other sources. This scenario excludes intervening cash flows and minimum operating cash needs and is not a current liquidity forecast. [F1, D1]

## Business Analyst

- The largest customer accounts for 35% of revenue. Conditional inference: losing that customer could materially reduce sales if its purchases cease and cannot be replaced; the evidence does not establish the likelihood of loss or contractual protections. [B1]
- Conditional inference: industrial customer exposure could transmit a downturn in customer demand to ABC's orders, assuming customer purchases fluctuate with industrial activity. The supplied profile does not establish ABC's actual cyclicality or downturn resilience. [B1]
